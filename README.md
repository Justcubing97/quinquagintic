# What is Quinquagintic?
Quinquagintic is my first real incremental game. There exist three planned Domains: Decimal, Integer, and Infinity. Each one is focused on a different type of progression:

Decimal: Simple mechanics, one for each reset layer, 11 layers

Integer: Intricate reset layers, often having more than one mechanic (almost like IMR's reset layers), 8 layers

Infinity: Mechanics and progression are confusing; only a few layers (3-5?)

HOWEVER, this game, as of writing, is only in the Decimal Domain.

# Tips for QoL! (and some notations!)

- Clicking any point label on the top (NP, OP, SP, etc.) will reset if possible.
- To understand some notations, #U% means #-type Upgrade % (selecting a specific one), #U%-% means #-type Upgrade %-% (selecting from a range), #U%, % means #-type Upgrade %, % (selecting two or more), and #U# / #Us means all #-type Upgrades. If there is no specification after an upgrade type, assume all.
- For Challenge Notations, C# means Challenge # and C#x% means % completions of Challenge #.

# Give me a description of Quinquagintic (guide).
Warning, there are spoilers ahead!

## Decillionth Section
This is the simplest section of them all. Here, there are two upgrades. DU1 increases your N gain by 2 (2.5 if you Nonillionth Reset'd), and DU2 multiplies your gain by 1.4, although it has a cap of 10 buys. You reach the next section with 1e-30 N.

## Nonillionth Section
You get to reset your DS progress for some juicy Nonillionth Points, along with boosting DU1 and multiplying N by 5!

Here, there are two columns of upgrades: the infinites and the one-times. NU1 and NU3 are the infinites, doubling NP gain and tripling N gain respectively, while NU2 and NU4 are the one-times, setting DU1's requirement base to 4e-33 and lowering the scaling of NU1 and NU2 respectively. You reach the next section with 1e-27 N.

UPDATE: NU3 is no longer infinite due to causing runaway inflation at high NP levels. It has been capped to 8 buys.

## Octillionth Section
After Octillionth Resetting, you can gain Octillionth Points (OP) in exchange for ALL YOUR PROGRESS SO FAR! But...

Now you get to play with Challenges! Upon first reset, you get to automate DU# and NU#, so now you can close those sections. There's also an x4 boost to NP upon first reset. HOWEVER, get at least 5 Octillionth Points before attempting any challenge, not even C4. You also get to see the two sides of the lore of this game, Justcubing97 (me!) and "The Entity."

Challenge 1, Hyperinflation, squares all scalings, so that the difficult x10 scaling of NU3 is jacked up to an x100! (No, silly, not 100 factorial, the exclamation mark is purely for effect.) However, every C1 completion adds 0.5 to OP's boost to N! Fun!

Challenge 2, Self-destruction, makes Number reduce its own gain. Eventually, it will slow down enough to cause a minuscule difference. But in return, every completion will add 0.5 to the OP gain multiplier!

Challenge 3, No Nonillionths, is pretty self-explanatory. NP is disabled, and that text that says "at an increased cost" just means that it will NOT cost the initial value of NP to get OP. Because it affects NP, like C2, it will add 0.5 to the NP gain this time, per completion.

Finally, Challenge 4, Not so OP now (haha funny pun, OP = overpowered and Octillionth Points), nullifies the OP boost to N. You probably wouldn't get to 1e-26 without OP's boost, so I recommended that you not touch ANY of these challenges until 5 OP, and DEFINITELY NOT C4 until you get enough completions of C3 (C1 and C2's reward are useless in C4). However, each C4 completion adds 0.05 to N's base. You don't actually need to complete C4 to get to the next section

Finally, Challenge Completions (CC) are just the total number of challenges completed. CC Milestones (CCM) are unlocked at 3, 10, and 20 CC. After CCM1, each CC gives 0.5 to the multiplier of OP, after CCM2, each CC will give 0.25 to N's multiplier, and CCM3 unlocks the next section.

## Septillionth Section
Welcome to the Atomic realm. After Septillionth Resetting you get to gain Atoms! Also, Septillionth Resetting WILL reset your Challenge Completions, however you keep the Septillionth Section unlocked. Septillionth Resetting also gives you a x2 boost to NP and OP. You're met with an infobox that explains the inspiration for the Atoms mini incremental, along with the fact that SP doesn't have any good use... not yet, at least.

Anyway, you get to see all kinds of properties about Atoms, including how much you have (both in the section AND in the top bar), your gain, the current formula, the boost to N, and any boosts to Atoms (A).

And then there's the six currently atom upgrades, or AU1-6. Some boost the additive part of the formula, some boost the multiplicative part of the formula. Use the new multiplier to get to the next section.

## Sextillionth Section
After playing with atomic particles and getting quite a few of them, it's time to Sextillionth Reset! Doing this will give you the most boosts BY FAR: x10 NP, OP, and SP, x2 CC, x4 SP scaling, x5 Atoms, an improved Atoms formula, and kept Challenges, along with Sextillionth Points (SXP), which... wait. Where can you use these?

IT'S TIME FOR THE FIRST UPGRADE TREE! Right off the bat, you'll see that some upgrades, like SXUT12, are marked with ???. THIS IS INTENTIONAL. (In the future, I may change this to make them hidden until their unlock, so this sentence will be false.) Also, some upgrades cost SXP, others REQUIRE CC (I'm not stealing your Challenge Completions), and some require Atoms.

There are three types of existing upgrades in the Sextillionth Upgrade Tree (SXUT; SXUT#% means Sextillionth Upgrade Tree Row #, Column %): static boost, varying boost, and unlocks.

SXUT11, SXUT21, SXUT23, SXUT32, SXUT41, and SXUT43 are of the first type, giving a static, or constant boost to currencies or boosts. These can range from x2 all the way to x10B (you'll see why when you get to the SXUT).

SXUT22 and SXUT33 are of the second type, varying boost. This means that the boost to the currencies and/or boosts change based on other variables. Since there are only two here, I'll go ahead and explain. SXUT22 makes C3's reward affect Atoms directly, and SXUT33 makes SXP (+1) multiply Atoms as well.

SXUT31 and SXUT42 are of the unlock type. These two... well... unlock stuff. SXUT31 unlocks two more AUs (AU7 and AU8, found in the Atoms tab) and keeps DU and NU upgrades, while SXUT42 unlocks Challenge 5.

Challenge 5 sort of mirrors Challenge 3 in the sense that you cannot gain ANY POINT TYPES, but only SXP and the SXUT is kept as a Sextillionth Reset is forced upon entry and exit. The goal of C5 is to get... 1 octovigintillion atoms (1e+87). I'm done. JUST KIDDING! Use the Atoms incremental and MAKE SURE YOU HAVE SXUT43 BEFORE ENTRY. After that, there are a LOT of timewalls in this Challenge. I estimate 8-10 minutes, though I use an autoclicker. Beating this challenge will unlock the Quintillionth Reset and give a x5 boost to N. As of 0.4.3, completing this Challenge is the endgame. But it's not 0.4.3.

## Quintillionth Section
Welcome to the halfway point! Here, you have five layers ahead of you, and five behind you. It's time to Quintillionth Reset, which is sad, meaning YOU LOSE ALL OF YOUR PROGRESS NO! But in return, you get x3 Number gain, automated AUs, and... you can keep SXUT31? Yeah, that one allowed you to keep your upgrades AND AU7-8!

Well, Quintillionth Points (QP) generates Quintillionth Fragments (QF), and getting 10 QF is the endgame for 0.5.
